Why The Plug

A festival built for the block, not just on it.

The Plug brings resources, jobs, wellness, and joy into one free day in Prince George's County — no cost to attend, no barrier to walk through the gate. Local vendors sell alongside employers hiring on the spot, county services that people actually need, and a stage that sounds like home.

It started in Largo, and it stays rooted there on purpose.

"I'm investing in the place that invested in me."

Cal Wade Jr. — Founder, Wade Ventures Group

The Zones

Eight areas, one lot.

Every zone does one job well. Walk the whole lot and you've covered vendors, jobs, wellness, family, tech, and food.

ZONE A
The Market
Local vendors, makers & small businesses — Harvest & Hustle.
ZONE B
Main Stage
DJs, live performances & community spotlight.
ZONE C
Opportunity Zone
Employers, workforce programs & financial literacy.
ZONE D
Wellness Village
Free health screenings, mental health & housing support.
ZONE E
Family Corner
Kids' activities, face painting & giveaways.
ZONE F
Tech Zone
Free WiFi, device giveaways & digital literacy workshops.
ZONE G
Employer Hub
On-the-spot hiring & career programs.
ZONE H
Food Truck Row
3–5 trucks lining the south edge, from Metro entry up.
